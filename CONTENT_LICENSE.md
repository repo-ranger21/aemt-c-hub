# Content terms and medical disclaimer

The MIT license covers the app code. The study content in `src/data/`
(`drugs.json`, `topics.json`, `questions.json`) is covered by this file instead.

## Not for clinical use

**This is one student's exam-prep material. It is not a clinical reference, not
medical advice, and not a protocol.** Do not use it to make a patient care
decision.

Doses, scope markings, and protocol references in this repository may be wrong,
incomplete, or out of date. They were transcribed by hand and by AI assistance
from the sources listed below, and they have not been reviewed or approved by any
medical director, EMS agency, or certifying body.

If you are treating a patient, use your agency's current protocols and your
medical director's standing orders. Those are the only authority that counts.

## Sources

Content is **summarized and rewritten**, not reproduced. No copyrighted text,
images, slides, or exam items are redistributed here.

| Source | How it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Rhode Island Statewide EMS Protocols v2026.02 (RIDOH Center for EMS) | Public document. RI doses and scope are cited by protocol number (e.g. `3.06A`). |
| AHA / AAP 2025 Guidelines for CPR and ECC, Parts 6 and 8 | Recommendations summarized in plain language; algorithm figures not reproduced. |
| *Advanced Emergency Care and Transportation of the Sick and Injured* (AAOS/Jones & Bartlett) and its instructor slides | Concepts restated in original wording. No slide text, figures, or test-bank items. |
| *Introduction to Basic Cardiac Dysrhythmias* and its lecture slides | Rhythm criteria restated in original wording. ECG strips in the app are drawn from scratch as SVG, not scanned. |

Source files themselves (textbook slide decks, the protocol PDF) are **not** in
this repository. `/docs` is gitignored for that reason.

No recalled or reconstructed NREMT exam items appear here. NREMT items are secure
test content, and using them puts a candidate's certification at risk.

## What you may do with the content

You may read it, learn from it, fork it, and adapt it for your own studying.

If you redistribute it in any form, keep this file with it, and keep the
not-for-clinical-use warning intact. Do not present it as an authoritative
protocol reference or as approved by any agency.
